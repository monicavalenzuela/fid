Welcome to Formé

I'm a hairstylist with over 28 years of experience, and I believe great hair should enhance your natural features and reflect your personal style. My background as a former Redken educator and Redken Certified Hair Colorist continues to influence my approach to hair design, technique, and continued growth as an artist. I enjoy working across all aspects of hair, from thoughtful haircuts and dimensional color to styling that feels modern, balanced, and effortless. My approach is always intentional, with an emphasis on movement, versatility, and a polished finish. I believe healthy hair and preserving its integrity are essential to beautiful, lasting results. I take the time to understand each client's goals and create a look that feels authentic to them. My goal is for every client to leave feeling confident, cared for, and like the best version of themselves.
